Build a production-grade Agentic AI Legal Assistant specialized for Burkina Faso laws and regulations.

The objective is to build one of the most reliable legal AI systems possible.

The priority order is:

1. Accuracy
2. Reliability
3. Explainability
4. Traceability
5. Low hallucination
6. Latest information
7. Fast response

The system must NEVER answer like a generic chatbot.

It must behave like an expert legal research assistant.

## Additional recommendations

To make this truly state of the art, I'd add a few capabilities beyond your original list:

* **Knowledge freshness pipeline:** Automatically monitor official Burkina Faso legal sources, detect document changes, version legal texts, and trigger incremental re-indexing.
* **Authority-weighted evidence ranking:** Assign trust scores (e.g., Constitution > amended law > decree > ministerial circular > official press release > news article) and use these scores during conflict resolution.
* **Grounded answer policy:** Every substantive legal statement should be traceable to one or more cited sources. If evidence is insufficient, the system should explicitly say so instead of guessing.
* **Legal timeline reasoning:** When laws have amendments or effective dates, reason over the applicable version based on the user's scenario date.
* **Human review mode:** For low-confidence or high-risk legal questions, allow escalation to a legal expert rather than returning a definitive answer.
* **Multi-language support:** Since Burkina Faso uses French officially but users may ask in English or local languages, separate retrieval language from response language so evidence can be retrieved in French while answers are generated in the user's preferred language.

With these additions, the project evolves from a legal chatbot into a **production-grade Legal AI Research Platform** suitable for government agencies, law firms, enterprises, and public legal information services.
